# R11: the real-case screen did not admit a replacement

Report/discussion date: 2026-11-02, Europe/Berlin. Stage: G1 admission screen.
Predecessor: [R10](10-source-backed-state-model.md). Spec:
[constraint-guided compression](../../../.kiro/specs/constraint-guided-compression/brief.md).

## Outcome

No real-code replacement was admitted or executed. Selective reading and a
read-only census did not establish an untried responsibility with a concrete,
useful deletion hypothesis. The earlier screening bar of at least 25% and 30
net production statements was retained. There is **no measured saving**, not
a measured zero-saving candidate.

This is a discovery failure within a manual sample. It does not show that these
repositories are minimal or uncompressible. It also means the promised isolated
replacement trial could not responsibly start: the required target was not
established. Full feasibility remains **revise**.

## Why the apparent opportunities did not qualify

Lesscode's analyzer wrappers look repetitive, but their stderr policy, exception
sets, artifact selection and syntax-validation behavior differ. Sharing one
worker could be useful local maintenance work; a substantial complete-scope
deletion has not been established. Its strict readers and outcome classifiers
also overlap earlier trials that produced small or negative reductions.

NPU's CLI configuration already has a field table and shared precedence
resolver. Its renderers already share result preparation. Its SQLite store has
repeated locking/error shapes, but transaction boundaries and file-versus-store
diagnostics differ. None supplied a credible qualifying replacement after
counting new helpers and retained behavior. These observations are not proof
that further simplification is impossible.

Facdrone's historical configuration coupling remains a code-level lead, but
deleting retained schema behavior would change the contract. The cache needs
code/data/config identity and replay evidence before replacement; hardware and
performance remain outside scope. ATS's previously successful bilingual report
trial was not relabeled as a new discovery; its parser has distinct extraction
lifecycles and already data-driven section detection.

Repository dependency manifests were inspected where relevant; this is not a
completed installed-capability inventory for any admitted candidate. No new
dependency, analysis framework, query pack or modeling service was built.

## Consequence

The unresolved bottleneck is now **selecting a substantial removable mechanism**,
not demonstrating another state-model fixture. R10's passing model checks must
not be promoted into a compression result. More CodeQL precision, solver
features or agent orchestration would not supply the missing deletion hypothesis
by themselves.

Keep these leads with their disposition. A next efficacy attempt must identify
the old implementation that disappears and the complete replacement cost before
freezing tests or building another model. A known-positive replay can calibrate
the new modeling procedure, but must be labeled calibration and cannot establish
new discovery efficacy or count prior savings twice. Do not continue the same
unproductive screen indefinitely or silently relax the usefulness criterion.

No candidate patch, behavioral comparison, CodeQL run, SMT run or native
comparison occurred at this stage. No production or external specimen was
changed. The [prototype gate](../../../.kiro/specs/constraint-guided-compression/prototype-gate.md)
and production task approvals remain unchanged. This entry records a negative
admission result rather than claiming a completed real-code experiment.
