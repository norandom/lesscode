## 1. Protection level, in plain terms

The candidate is protected to exactly this extent and no further (requirement 10.2):

1. **The acceptance corpus**: 50 recorded sequences, 317 operations over the 24-operation alphabet (`admit, admit_durable,
   admitted, baseline_view, census_references, cli_enroll, cli_readings, cli_retire, enroll, events, flag, ledger,
   live_evidence, open_flags, overdue_flags, readings, retire, run, seams, seed_evidence, standing, standing_view, tick,
   transition`; `lifecycle_review` excluded), replayed on the candidate with zero Level 1 divergence and zero Level 2 pairs
   (turn 3, `e4-t3-arm`).
2. **The 12 seed test files** with one deselection (the event id under a non-UTC session time zone) and one plugin skip
   (the operator-tier rule): root group 108 passed, 1 skipped, 1 deselected; research group 1 passed, on the candidate.
3. **The held-out corpus**: 74 sequences, 424 operations, recorded after acceptance from a seed that did not exist before
   (20261107; 26 seed sequences plus 48 generated, 47 of them new sequences in operation terms, 1 an exact duplicate),
   replayed on the candidate with zero Level 1 divergence and zero Level 2 pairs (`e6-replay-cand`).
4. **The measured sensitivity of that acceptance arm**: 19 of 28 competent sampled mutants killed on the candidate
   (0.679; 30 draws, seed 20261106, population 1,488) and 12 of 29 on the original (0.414; population 1,468), R22
   convention, bar 0.70 not met on either tree. Three of the four hand-written semantic mutants caught on both trees (M2
   by a seed test, M3 and M4 by the corpus at the predicted sequence and operation); M1 missed on both.
5. **The independent review**: 128 probes executed on both trees, 102 agreeing and 26 disagreeing in six findings F1 to
   F6; the probes are evidence about their inputs only.
6. **The differential outcome within its bounds**: void under plan kill criterion 4 (the `window` planted-mutant solver
   control did not reach its planted difference in 5,559 iterations). The two realised solver counterexamples
   (`compare`, `standing`) stand as evidence of two oracle-blind differences and are not credited to the step.

Together: on every recorded input and every seed test on this host, the candidate does what the original does. Off those
inputs, the measured sensitivity says that about one competent fault in three at exercised sites survives the arm, and
the review and the differential found seven concrete differences on error and malformed-input paths. That is the whole
claim.

### The Level 2 observation on original #15

The acceptance arm scores Level 1 only (plan 6.4). The recorded SQL text and parameters (Level 2) are collected on every
replay, and on `e4o-m15` they differ in 9 operations over the 50 sequences (`decision_evidence.scope <= %(scope_1)s`
against the recorded `scope = %(scope_1)s`, same parameters; note `e4o-m15-level2-observation`). The mutant stays
survived under the plan. What this says for the protection statement: the recorded SQL would catch a widened predicate
that the dumped rows cannot distinguish, so a Level 2 acceptance condition would raise the protection on query shape
without a new recording; it would also turn every harmless query rewrite into a divergence, which is why E3 and E4 kept
Level 2 as a differential input rather than an acceptance condition. Neither reading changes a verdict.

## 4. The kill rates, with the composition caveat

| Tree | Killed | Survived | Incompetent | Rate (R22) | Bar 0.70 | Post hoc (labelled, not the result) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| original (`e4o`) | 12 | 17 | 1 | 12/29 = 0.414 | not met | 0.571 without the 8 annotation-only survivors; 0.800 without those and the 6 residue-sited |
| candidate (`e4c`) | 19 | 9 | 2 | 19/28 = 0.679 | not met | 0.679 (no equivalent survivor); 0.864 without the 6 residue-sited |

The two rates come from the same seed, draw rule, arm, classification and restore-and-verify, but from different
populations over different code (requirement 8.6): the 11 `lifecycle_group` draws are the same specs on both trees and
classify identically; the other 19 differ. The original's 0.414 is a result about its sample's composition, stated in
advance (ceiling 15/29 = 0.517 with 14 expected survivors; 13 of them survived and #28 was killed against expectation):
8 of its 30 draws are annotation-only (#11, 13, 14, 20, 21, 22, 23, 27), and of the 6 draws the plan expected to
survive in the declared residue or in `study_baseline` (#5, 6, 8, 26, 28, 29) five survived (#5, 6, 8, 26, 29) and #28
was killed; #2 (`_pod_of`, patched away by every path, never declared residue) survived as a closed assumption. On the
candidate no draw is annotation-only; of the 6 expected survivors (#5, 6, 8 in `lifecycle_review`, #28, 29 in root
`lifecycle_store`, #27 in `study_baseline`) four survived (#5, 6, 8, 27) and #28, #29 were killed; #2 and #11
(`bind_lifecycle`, undeclared) survived as closed assumptions. Thirty draws carry no significance; the pair says the arm is not less sensitive on the candidate than on the original,
and nothing finer. Neither tree reaches 0.70, and the five real survivors on exercised code are corpus gaps shared in
kind with E3's (malformed or boundary inputs the recorded traces are thinnest on).

## 5. What nobody may read E4 as protecting

- Not equivalence: zero divergence is a statement about 50 plus 74 recorded sequences and 12 seed files, under one
  alphabet of 24 operations over at most two starting states and sequences of 3 to 8 operations, on one host.
- Not the residue above: `lifecycle_admit`, `lifecycle_review`, `pod_lifecycle_standing`, `bind_lifecycle` and the
  unpatched bodies of `_pod_of` and `study_baseline` have never run under the arm on either tree; a candidate could
  differ there arbitrarily and still be accepted.
- Not the seven input classes above and the six survivors' input classes: on those the trees are known or shown to be
  able to differ, and the oracle cannot see them.
- Not the `window` pair: an unresolved differential target (corpus agrees on 300 inputs, solver inconclusive, verdict
  not read), never read as equivalent.
- Not production equivalence of error handling: F1 is a behaviour change on a malformed input the types forbid, which
  is the kind of input a production fault produces.
- Not a sensitivity of 0.70: the bar the plan carried from E3 is unmet on both trees, and the candidate's 0.679 is in
  the predicted band only because the band was set from E3's measured 0.643.
- Not adoption, not a patch for Facdrone, not a statement about any other scope, round, regenerator, model or language,
  and not determinism of the regenerator (the endpoint exposes no seed).
